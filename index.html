Frequently Asked Questions

Good to know before we begin.

How long does a website take to build?

It depends on the scope, content and feedback turnaround. An estimated timeline will be included in your proposal.

Are domain and hosting included in the price?

Development fees and recurring domain, hosting or database costs are separate unless specifically included in the proposal. All recurring costs will be explained before work begins.

Can you build features specifically for my business?

Yes. We'll assess your requirements, complexity, security needs and budget before confirming the scope and price.

How do I start a project?

Contact us with a brief description of your business, the problem you want to solve and the features you need. We'll discuss the scope and prepare a proposal.
