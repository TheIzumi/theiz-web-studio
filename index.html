Frequently Asked Questions

Before we get started.

How much does a website cost?

Business websites start from RM600, while booking systems start from RM1,500. Final pricing depends on the agreed scope, functionality and complexity.

How long will my project take?

Timelines depend on the project size, content, revisions and technical requirements. A timeline will be discussed before development begins.

Will there be ongoing costs?

A domain name, hosting, database, third-party services or maintenance may involve recurring fees. Any applicable costs should be discussed before the project starts.

Can you build a system specifically for my business?

Custom systems can be planned around your workflow. The available features, security requirements, integrations and budget will determine the solution.

How do I request a quotation?

Contact TheIz Web Studio through WhatsApp or email. Briefly describe your business, the type of project you need and the features you have in mind.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You explain what your business needs to achieve. We can discuss the technical approach and the information required to plan the project.
